## How Noeau OS Works

Each **Agent** is a named AI persona with a specific role. When you open an agent file, you get:
1. A clear **mission** describing what the agent does
2. A **prompt block** you can paste directly into an AI chat
3. A **log section** to record outputs

Use **Templates** to create consistent notes for learning, research, daily planning, and weekly reviews. Each template is designed to feed naturally into the relevant agent.
